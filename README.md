# chestiny

So you want to contribute to the Chess ARG? Here's how to get started.

## Prerequesite Collect the chess pieces and solve the 27 chess puzzles
If you haven't already, you need to collect the 60 chess pieces first.  360GameTV has a [great vid for this](https://youtu.be/0DLkaOwYXgU).

Once you've collected, you'll need to do the 27 chess puzzles. Another [vid from 360GameTV for this.](https://youtu.be/Nf9Di_czvM0)

## Submitting Boards and Frequency Codes
After you've solved the 27 chess puzzles, the area that held the "take" action will spwawn a water fountain that you can interact with.  If you interact with that, it will give you a mini cutscene with a board state.  Take a screen capture of this!  You'll also see a buff on the lower left that reads "signal frequency xZZZZ" where ZZZZ is a number.

Steps to upload
1. Upload your screenshot to [imgur.com](https://imgur.com/).  Helps if you update the description to the frequency code
2. Go to https://tjl.co/queens-gambit-arg/ and fill in the form.  Make sure you include a link to your screenshot from imgur

Something to note: a new frequency code is generated for you every hour, at 10 to the hour for each of your characters!  So you can see and submit 3 codes every hour.  Also, once you've completed the initial 27 puzzles, you can always come back to the fountain from the director by going to Last City -> Hidden Antechamber -> Endgame

## Submitting Fen Codes and AION Archives
Part of the boards included parts of a QR code.  The community has solved that an it revealed aion-archives.net.  If you go to that site, you can enter various numbers to get some junk, some inside jokes, some lore, and more importantly tracking the status of various frequency codes.

How to submit your Fen code and contributing the status
1. Go to https://www.chess.com/analysis?tab=analysis and click "Set Up Position"
2. Clear the board by clicking the trash can icon
3. Replicate the board state by matching the pieces and color of your screenshot
4. Once you're done, you'll see a fen code that looks like "pRbNBbpp/b6p/7P/P6B/N6N/k6p/q6B/RQrnbbPN" (that's just an example).  You'll want to copy the string leading up to but not including the space character.
5. Go to aion-archives.net
6. Enter your frequency code as four digits.  For example, if your code was 1234, you'd enter that.  If your code was 24, pad with leading zeroes so then you'd enter 0024
7. Paste your fen code.  Use the paste button on the keypad onscreen as ctrl-v doesn't work.
8. You'll see some text on screen
9. Look at the instructions at https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/d/1_HoeI57Y1xkMv4UsrjLC2iuhHWI13g5sxc7JhrSp1Gk/ (Manual Data Entry Tab).
10. Enter your data there
